Biography

Dr. Tapankumar Trivedi is an electrical engineering academic with over 17 years of extensive teaching, research, and curriculum leadership experience. He currently serves as an Associate Professor in the Department of Electrical Engineering at Marwadi University, Rajkot. He earned his Ph.D. from Charotar University of Science and Technology in 2019, focusing on the Direct Power Control of Unified Power Quality Conditioners, and his M.Tech. from IIT Roorkee in 2012.

His research spans advanced power electronic converter design, microgrid control, and the integration of deep learning techniques for power quality diagnostics. He is the Principal Investigator for an active deep learning project funded by the Government of Gujarat and is an active reviewer for premier IEEE transactions. Dr. Trivedi is a Senior Member of IEEE.

About Me

Detailed Biography

Dr. Tapankumar Trivedi obtained his Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering from Charotar University of Science and Technology in 2019, successfully defending his thesis on the "Direct Power Control of Unified Power Quality Conditioner". Prior to his doctoral studies, he earned his Master of Technology (M.Tech.) from the prestigious Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Roorkee in 2012, specializing in Power Electronics and Electrical Drives, where his dissertation focused on Multiphase Multilevel Inverters for Induction Motor Drives.

Presently, he is an Associate Professor at Marwadi University, serving as the Section Head for the M.Tech. program in Power Electronics and Electrical Drives. His responsibilities extend beyond teaching to comprehensive curriculum development aligned with NBA and NAAC guidelines. He is an active researcher, having secured government grants as a Principal Investigator, and is dedicated to bridging the gap between theoretical knowledge and real-time hardware implementation.

Teaching Philosophy

My teaching philosophy aims to deliver rigorous yet accessible instruction in Power Electronics, Power Systems, and AI-Driven Energy Management at both undergraduate and postgraduate levels. I strongly believe in a pedagogical approach that integrates robust theoretical foundations with extensive hardware lab work—including Hardware-in-the-Loop (HIL) simulation and rapid control prototyping. This dual approach ensures that students are not only academically proficient but are also thoroughly prepared for dynamic research and industrial roles.

Research Vision

My near-term research agenda centers on three key pillars: (1) Intelligent power quality monitoring utilizing deep learning models for smart grids and industrial microgrids; (2) Advanced converter control strategies to facilitate seamless renewable energy integration; and (3) AI-assisted fault detection mechanisms for power electronic drives, specifically aimed at electric vehicle (EV) applications. I continuously seek to pursue national funding (DST-SERB, GCSST) and international collaborations to establish a competitive, forward-thinking research group.

Power Electronics & Direct Power Control

Description: Design and advanced control of highly efficient power conversion systems. This involves sliding mode-based direct power control, multilevel inverters, and unified power quality conditioners.
